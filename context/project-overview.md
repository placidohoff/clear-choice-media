**Website Redesign + SEO Competitive Blueprint**
*Prepared for web designer/developer • August 2026*
Reference websites: Mercury Photo Lounge and Pozie Booths. Use them for design and user-experience inspiration only; do not copy their wording, branding, layout, or content.

# 1. Business Positioning

Clear Choice Media is a Rhode Island-based event media company providing Event Photography, Event Video, Photo Booth Experiences, 360 Photo Booth, Corporate Event Content, Brand Activations, Social Media Event Content, and Drone/Aerial Services where appropriate.
Primary positioning: Event Media + Experiences. The brand should feel like a premium event production/content company rather than only a traditional photographer or photo booth rental company.
Core message: WE CAPTURE THE ENERGY.
The customer should immediately understand that Clear Choice Media helps businesses, couples, organizations, and event hosts capture, entertain, and extend the life of their events through professional photography, video, and interactive photo booth experiences.

# 9. One Event. One Media Team.

This is a major differentiator. Clear Choice can potentially provide Photography + Video + Photo Booth under one company.
Core positioning: ONE EVENT. MORE WAYS TO EXPERIENCE IT.
Do not make 'Event Media & Experiences' the primary Google keyword phrase. Use it as brand positioning. Use search language such as event photography, photo booth rental, and event video for SEO.

# 10. Local SEO & Venue Strategy

The website should support Clear Choice Media's Google Business Profile. Keep business information and services consistent across the website and major listings.
Build real venue/event case studies only where Clear Choice has actually worked or can legitimately provide useful information. Examples can include event photography at The Strand Ballroom in Providence and other real venues.
Useful venue pages can combine real location context with actual Clear Choice work, services, photos, and event details.

# 12. Technical SEO Requirements

- Unique title tag for every page
- Unique meta description
- One clear H1 per page
- Proper H2/H3 structure
- Descriptive URLs
- Compressed/optimized images
- Descriptive image filenames
- Descriptive image alt text
- Strong internal linking
- XML sitemap
- Google Search Console
- Google Analytics
- Relevant LocalBusiness/ProfessionalService and Organization structured data
- Breadcrumbs where useful
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Fast page loading and Core Web Vitals optimization
- HTTPS
- Canonical URLs
- 301 redirects for changed/removed URLs

# 13. Booking Flow

Every major page needs a clear CTA.

- CHECK AVAILABILITY
- REQUEST A QUOTE

Recommended form fields: Name, Email, Phone, Event Date, Event Type, Event Location, Services Interested In, Estimated Guest Count, Additional Details.
Connect the booking flow to the existing Clear Choice Media booking system where possible.

# 14. Image & Video Strategy

Use actual Clear Choice Media work throughout the website. Prioritize real guests, real events, real photo booth interactions, corporate events, behind-the-scenes content, event details, lighting, finished images, 360 footage, and video stills.
Homepage reel target: 15–30 seconds, fast-paced, works muted, and shows event arrival, camera action, guests, booth activity, prints, 360, dancing, corporate events, finished photographs, and Clear Choice Media branding.

# 15. Mobile Experience

- Mobile design is a priority.
- Keep CHECK AVAILABILITY, CALL, and GET A QUOTE easy to access.
- Use optimized images/video.
- Keep copy concise and readable.
- Do not allow video or animations to destroy page speed.

# 16. What NOT to Do

- Do not make every page a giant wall of SEO text.
- Do not stuff 'Providence RI' into every sentence.
- Do not create dozens of fake city pages.
- Do not rely on stock photography.
- Do not copy competitor wording or branding.
- Do not make the homepage extremely long before showing actual work.
- Do not make visitors hunt for the booking button.
- Do not make Clear Choice look cheap just because you are trying to rank.
